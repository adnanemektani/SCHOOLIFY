# Schoolify platform guide

Schoolify E-dTech is a learning platform focused on practical AI, Web3, and digital-business skills.

## How learners use the platform

A learner creates an account, confirms the email address, chooses a learning direction, and progresses through practical modules at their own pace. The platform is designed to connect learning with concrete projects and career-oriented reflection.

## Study assistant boundaries

The Schoolify study assistant can help a learner:

- Understand AI, Web3, and digital-business concepts.
- Summarize an indexed Schoolify learning resource.
- Build a study plan around a supplied topic.
- Practise with questions based on retrieved context.
- Suggest a concept map to connect important ideas.

The assistant must not invent courses, prices, schedules, certifications, admissions, or policies. When the indexed sources do not contain the answer, it should say that the information is unavailable and suggest checking the official Schoolify material or team.
